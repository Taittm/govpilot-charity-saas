# Data Processing Agreement (Template)

**Status:** Template drafted and ready to issue. Not yet signed with any client — this
prototype has no real clients. "DPAs are in place with existing clients" (the Phase 7
target) can only be true once real client organisations exist and this document (reviewed
by a solicitor before real-world use) has been issued and countersigned.

*Not legal advice. This is a standard-shape UK GDPR Article 28 processor agreement
covering the obligations a controller (the client) and processor (the platform) owe each
other. Have it reviewed by a solicitor before issuing it to a real client.*

## 1. Subject matter and duration

This agreement covers the Processor's processing of personal data on the Controller's
behalf via the Compliance Hub platform, for the duration of the Controller's
subscription plus any period required to fulfil clause 8 (return/deletion of data).

## 2. Nature and purpose of processing

The Processor stores and processes personal data submitted by the Controller in order to
provide compliance management services: trustee/director records, donor and Gift Aid
records, subject access request logs, health check responses, and associated documents.

## 3. Categories of data subjects

Trustees/directors, staff, volunteers, donors, and individuals who are the subject of a
subject access request logged by the Controller.

## 4. Types of personal data

Names, contact details, roles, DBS check dates and types (not DBS check results/content),
donation records, and any personal data the Controller includes in free-text fields
(policy notes, breach descriptions, grant notes, etc.) — the Controller is responsible for
not entering special category data into free-text fields beyond what's necessary.

## 5. Processor obligations

The Processor shall:

- process personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions (including
  regarding international transfers), unless required to do otherwise by law;
- ensure persons authorised to process the data are subject to confidentiality;
- implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures (see the
  Processor's data retention policy and backup/disaster recovery plan);
- not engage a sub-processor without the Controller's prior authorisation, and impose the
  same data protection obligations on any sub-processor it does engage;
- assist the Controller in responding to data subject rights requests;
- assist the Controller with its obligations around security, breach notification, and
  data protection impact assessments;
- at the Controller's choice, delete or return all personal data at the end of the
  agreement, and delete existing copies unless the law requires storage;
- make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance
  with this clause, and allow for audits.

## 7. Data breach notification

The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any case within
[72] hours of becoming aware, of any personal data breach affecting the Controller's data,
providing the information the Controller needs to meet its own notification obligations to
the ICO and affected data subjects.

## 8. Return or deletion of data

On termination, the Processor will, at the Controller's written request, either return all
personal data in a commonly-used format or securely delete it, within [30] days, except
where retention is required by law.
